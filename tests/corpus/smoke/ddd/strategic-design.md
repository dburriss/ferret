# Strategic design

Domain-Driven Design (DDD) splits the work into strategic and tactical parts. Strategic design is about the big picture: which parts of the business deserve the best engineers, and how the system is carved into models.

## Core, supporting and generic subdomains

The core domain is where the company differentiates itself. Supporting subdomains are necessary but not special, and generic subdomains (payroll, authentication) should be bought or taken off the shelf. Spend modelling effort in proportion to strategic value.

## Why it matters

Without this step teams model everything with equal care, and the interesting part of the business gets lost in a sea of CRUD screens.
