# Context mapping

A context map shows how separate models relate and who depends on whom. Each relationship has a pattern.

## Patterns

- **Partnership**: two teams succeed or fail together.
- **Customer/supplier**: the downstream team negotiates needs with the upstream team.
- **Conformist**: downstream accepts upstream's model as is.
- **Shared kernel**: a small, jointly owned piece of model.
- **Open host service** with a **published language** for many consumers.

## Use

Drawing the map exposes political and technical coupling that no architecture diagram shows.
