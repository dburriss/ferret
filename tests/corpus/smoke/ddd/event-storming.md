# Event storming

Event storming is a workshop format, popular in DDD circles, where developers and domain experts put orange sticky notes for domain events on a long wall in time order.

## Steps

1. Chaotic exploration: everyone writes events.
2. Enforce the timeline and spot gaps.
3. Add commands, actors and policies.
4. Draw lines around clusters; these are candidate aggregates and contexts.

## Outcome

Disagreements surface quickly, and the shared picture on the wall becomes the seed of the shared vocabulary.
